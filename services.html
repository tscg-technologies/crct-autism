01
EVERYDAY SKILLS

Occupational Therapy

Occupational Therapy supports children in developing the skills they use in everyday life. Through suitable activities, children can practise coordination, independence and participation in daily routines.

Areas we focus on

  • Fine motor skills and hand coordination
  • Self-care and daily living activities
  • Hand-eye coordination
  • Play and everyday participation
Enquire About This Therapy →
02
POSITIVE DEVELOPMENT

Behavioral Therapy

Behavioral Therapy focuses on understanding a child's behavior and supporting positive ways to learn, communicate and interact with others.

Areas we focus on

  • Positive behavior development
  • Social interaction skills
  • Following instructions and routines
  • Learning and everyday participation
Enquire About This Therapy →
03
COMMUNICATION

Speech Therapy

Speech Therapy supports children in developing communication skills and expressing their thoughts, feelings and everyday needs through words, gestures and other forms of communication.

Areas we focus on

  • Speech and language development
  • Understanding and using words
  • Expressing thoughts and needs
  • Communication through words and gestures
Enquire About This Therapy →
04
SENSORY EXPERIENCES

Sensory Integration Therapy

Sensory Integration Therapy uses carefully selected activities to support how children experience and respond to sensations such as touch, sound and movement.

Areas we focus on

  • Exploring different textures and materials
  • Movement and balance activities
  • Responding to sensory experiences
  • Participation in everyday activities
Enquire About This Therapy →
05
INDIVIDUAL LEARNING

Special Education

Special Education provides learning support based on each child's abilities, interests and learning needs. Activities are planned to make learning more accessible and meaningful.

Areas we focus on

  • Early learning and foundational skills
  • Attention and learning activities
  • Basic academic concepts
  • Individual learning goals
