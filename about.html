ABOUT CRCT

About Carl Rogers Counselling and Therapy Centre

Every child has their own way of learning, growing and experiencing the world. At CRCT, we are committed to understanding each child and supporting families with care, patience and guidance.

A PLACE TO LEARN AND GROW

Who We Are

Carl Rogers Counselling and Therapy Centre is a place for children and families seeking support with child development, autism, counselling and therapy.

We take time to understand each child—their needs, strengths and pace—and support their development with care and patience.

Families are an important part of this journey. We aim to offer clear guidance and work alongside parents as they support their child.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To provide compassionate, child-focused support that helps children develop essential skills, build confidence and participate more comfortably in everyday life.

Our Vision

To create a supportive environment where every child is understood, encouraged and given opportunities to grow at their own pace.

CHILD-FOCUSED SUPPORT

Our Approach

We meet children with understanding and make room for families to be part of their growth.

Individual Attention

Understanding each child's unique needs and abilities.

Compassionate Care

Creating a comfortable and encouraging environment.

Family Involvement

Helping parents understand and support their child's development.

Continuous Growth

Encouraging children to develop skills at their own pace.
